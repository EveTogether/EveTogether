Runs overview · D v2 — split list + detail

Round 2 of variant D, after Jithran's feedback on v1. 1 · at 758 a clicked run opens as a drawer sliding in from the right; ✕, a click on the backdrop or Esc closes it. 2 · the activity strip stretches to the width it has instead of sitting small on the left. 3 · types and characters are filters in columns, with full names and portraits, filling the header's height. 4 · SUMMARY on the range line opens the summary with a DAY | MONTH switch — in the pane at 1303, in the same drawer at 758. The rest is v1's D unchanged. Design only — design/runs-overview/v2, made 2026-09-15. No repo code touched.
What is real and what is made up.
  • realEvery activity is one of the 177 saved activities in Jithran's own client.db (read-only copy, 2026-09-02 → 2026-09-14): site, type, system, start, duration, crew, ISK by source, enemy-type count, rewards, escalations and whether it reached ET. The six characters are his. The homefront crews of five, the 46-activity Thursday, the 12-hour Saturday are all as recorded. The portraits are his characters' own, from the EVE image server by character id, embedded in this file.
  • made upThe running run behind the "simulate" switch (Blood Refuge, 00:04:12). The days before 2 September are drawn as "not tracked yet" — there is no data there, real or otherwise.
  • Hover anything for its source or value. Letters: A available now · D derivable · N needs new capture. Switch annotations off for the clean app look.

1Summary · day and month v2

SUMMARY on the range line opens it: in the pane at 1303 (click again to go back to the selected run), in the 430 px drawer at 758 (✕, backdrop, Esc). A DAY | MONTH switch sits in its header. It starts on the view — a day picked in the strip opens DAY, a month opens MONTH — and resets when the view changes. Which day: the selected run's, else the day picked in the strip, else the newest day in view. Both use the same filters as the list. The month keeps ISK by source, by type, by character and top sites. The day has the same sections plus HOURS (24 cells shaded like the strip, by ISK or runs started in the hour) and TOP RUNS instead of top sites; click one to open it. D from the rows in view.

2Pane, activity selected

Everything the row abbreviates, plus the one PUBLISH button for this activity and OPEN DETAIL for ET-162's full screen. Selection is one click; the pane never steals it. At 758 the same pane is the drawer of note 3.

3758 · drawer v2

Clicking a run slides its detail in from the right over the whole window, like a site's menu, on a dimmed backdrop. It is 430 px wide, Jithran's choice (2026-09-15): the dimmed list stays visible beside it. Three ways out: ✕ in its top bar (32 px), a click anywhere on the backdrop, or Esc. The selected row stays highlighted under the backdrop, so closing puts you back where you were. With the drawer open ↑↓ step through the runs; with it closed ↵ opens it again. At 1303 nothing changed: the pane stays beside the list.

4Activity strip v2

A cell per day for the last twelve weeks, shaded in five steps by ISK (or by run count — the toggle under it). Days before the first tracked run are hatched: not tracked yet, never nothing flown. Click a day to see only that day; click it again, or ✕, for the whole month. v2: the strip stretches to the width it gets — the whole window at 758, a 400 px column at 1303 — so its cells are wider than tall rather than 11 px squares with empty space beside them. D from ActivitySummary alone.

5Range total

The same ActivitiesCount / NetFor formula the month bar and day bands use today, over whatever the strip and filters leave — so the strip, the total and the list can never disagree. The small bar splits the total by source; hover it. v2: PUBLISH n LOCAL moved onto this line; at 758 the totals take a second line.

6Filters v2

A block of columns per kind instead of two rows of chips. TYPES: icon, full name, count. CHARACTERS: portrait in the app's hex, full name, count — no initials to decode. A column is as wide as its longest name, so nothing truncates. The rows stretch to the height of the strip, so the band is filled instead of two short rows with space under them. A ticked box and the accent edge mark what is shown. All on by default; click hides, alt-click keeps only that one; show all appears per block once something is off. Counts follow the other filters. D

7Type icon

RunTypeCatalogue already gives every type an icon (ET-226/ET-74) and the row view-model carries it as TypeIcon — today's XAML never binds it. Here it does the work the dim TYPE word did. A

8Running

Six idle cards (120–207 px, six dead clocks) become one line: what runs, or nothing running, and one START RUN. Idle pilots are behind the button's picker, which is the same ET-221 dialog. Switch simulate a running run above to see a live lane.

9Server state

Published activities show a quiet cloud-check with the server's name. A local one reads local and grows a PUBLISH on hover or selection — the affordance is on every row, the button is not. A day band offers its local count in one go, and the range line the whole view. Auto-publish is on in Jithran's settings, so this is the exception path.

10Escalation

One chip → Mare Sargassum with the expiry in its tooltip, where today the same activity grows four chips: ESCALATION, ESCALATIONDUNGEONID, ESCALATIONSYSTEM, ESCALATIONEXPIRESATUTC (raw RunParameterKey names, GetActivityOverviewQueryHandler filters only the filament keys). Found in his real data, 21:42 on 14 Sep.

11Own share

The per-pilot ISK in an unfolded group: own bounty lines (RunBountyEntry per run, A) plus an even split of the homefront payout and mission rewards (D; the payout is per character counted in site anyway). Loot per pilot exists since ET-211 but is not joined here.

12ISK colours

Fixed inks per source, never the faction accent, so the bar reads the same in all four themes: bounty amber, loot blue, homefront payout green, mission rewards pink, mining violet (dataviz reference palette, validated on the panel surface).

13Portraits v2

His characters' portraits in the hex clip RunsWindow already uses, wherever a pilot appears: filters, crew stacks, crew tables. A crew member who is not one of his characters keeps initials. A portrait by character id.

14Contrast v2

Measured in all four themes (WCAG ratio, panel surface). Filter items: names ≥ 4.7:1 in every state, tick 7.3:1. Selected row: secondary text was dim, 3.3:1 on the Amarr fill; it now lifts to the text colour, ≥ 8.6:1 (hover ≥ 10.3:1). Drawer: an opaque panel over the backdrop, so its text keeps ≥ 4.8:1; its edge line stands 7.7:1 off the dimmed list.
