The biggest release since the beta: a map of New Eden, a RUNS screen with expected earnings, fleet abyssals and homefronts, game logs and killmails, a SKILLS module and stream overlays through the Local API. These are the highlights; every change is listed in the full changelog linked at the end.

### Map of New Eden
- A map of the whole of New Eden that can follow one of your characters (and draw where it has been) or your fleet, and can pop out into its own window.
- Set your in-game autopilot destination from the map, with Shift+click or a right-click for a waypoint. It needs a new, optional permission that each character allows once.
- The fleet map shows your fleet and its commander, and Fleet metrics has a map of its own.

### Runs, loot and expected earnings
- A RUNS screen with a calendar, an activity strip, a day, week and month summary, filters by type and character, and search by site, faction or type.
- Expected earnings: see what a site typically yields before and after a run, where a saved run landed among the runs of its site, and loot tables of what can drop and how often. Runs can be shared with your server so the figures rest on everyone's runs.
- Freeruns for a roam, ESS or officer hunting, escalations you can register, tie to a run and tick off in one click, and mining that shows per character and per ore.
- Run totals now agree on every screen, ISK figures show as whole numbers like in EVE, and consumables, filaments, blueprints and ammo are valued and accounted for properly.
- A compact run window, Ctrl+Shift+S to save the run you are flying, and a run left running when the app closed can be resumed.

### Abyssals and homefronts
- Abyssal rooms are found for you, you can start one yourself with NEW ROOM, and HIT QUALITY, TARGETS and ROOMS sections show how a run went.
- Fleet abyssals time every pilot on their own way in and out, and the commander can set one up before anyone goes in.
- Homefronts are recognised from the enemies you fight, with their own section, outcome and payout.

### Fleets
- Join a fleet run that is already going, see who is ready to fly, and have each fleet join runs automatically or never.
- Fleet runs publish themselves and your fleetmates' runs come in by themselves, with combat, kills, losses and mining shared along.
- Fleets, wings, squads and join requests update live for everyone, and a fleet can be stopped instead of only finished.

### Killmails, game logs and fits
- A KILLMAILS window with your own kills and losses, full detail per killmail, a map of where the ship died, and every loss linked to the run you lost it in.
- Game logs: the log lines of all your characters in one live list, read in more client languages.
- Fit browser and fit detail follow your changes live, and DPS, missile application and what-if savings are more accurate.

### Skills
- A SKILLS module with PLANS, OPTIMISE, doctrine minimums per fit, and SKILL IMPACT for the fit you are looking at, with three training targets.

### Stream overlays and Local API
- Eight ready-made stream overlays, a widget manager, and a Local API that serves your runs, latest kill or loss, repairs, neuts, cap and how well your weapons land. It no longer gives away where you are unless you ask it to, and OPSEC mode hides what you do not want to show.

### Server and updates
- The self-hosted server control panel gets a dashboard, lists per kind of data, backup and restore, and safer deletes. Servers and characters no longer lose their ESI sign-in to a network hiccup.
- EVE Together checks for updates while it runs and shows what changed, EVE Workbench is available as a second appraisal price source, and a guided setup helps you add your characters.
- Your data folder is now called `EveTogetherData`.
